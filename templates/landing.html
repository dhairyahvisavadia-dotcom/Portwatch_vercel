See exactly who's on your hotspot — and how, honestly

PortWatch is a live dashboard for a WiFi hotspot you're already connected to. It shows every connected device (phone, camera, speaker, laptop...), guesses its type with a stated confidence level, flags devices that might be sharing your connection onward, and scans nearby WiFi networks from your own laptop's radio — no third-party services, no passive packet capture, nothing running behind your back.

Built for a university project and demoed with real, defensible methodology — every detection technique documents exactly what it can and can't prove.

What this deliberately does not do

  • Does not connect to or access any network without your own credentials
  • Does not detect or identify devices that haven't joined a network you're connected to
  • Does not use monitor-mode capture, probe-request sniffing, or passive packet capture
  • Does not decrypt anyone's traffic or perform any attack against another device
