About this scan

This asks your laptop's own WiFi radio what networks it can hear broadcasting right now — the same thing your phone shows when you open its WiFi settings list. No internet connection needed, nothing sent anywhere, no account or API key required. This only shows networks currently in range of your laptop.

Distance is an estimate, not a measurement. Windows reports signal as a 0–100% quality value, which we convert to approximate dBm and run through the standard log-distance path-loss model (10²-based). Walls, interference, and antenna orientation can shift real distance significantly from this estimate — treat it as an order-of-magnitude band.
